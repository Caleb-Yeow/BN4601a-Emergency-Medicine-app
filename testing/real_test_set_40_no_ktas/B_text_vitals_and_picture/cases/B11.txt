A 36-year-old woman was admitted to the hospital for laparoscopic robotic hysterectomy and bilateral salpingectomy for high-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion found on screening pap smear and proven to be cervical intraepithelial neoplasia 2-3 on colposcopy. The patient was stable throughout surgery and was discharged home with no complications. The patient presented to emergency department 3 days after surgery with abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, and fever of 101 F. Upon presentation to ED, temperature was 37.5 C, heart rate 93 per minute, blood pressure 124/62, SpO2 of 95% on room air, respiratory rate of 20.
