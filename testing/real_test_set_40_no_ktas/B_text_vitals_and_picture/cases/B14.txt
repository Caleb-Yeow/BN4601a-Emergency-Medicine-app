An 83-year-old female with a past medical history of hypertension, type two diabetes mellitus, hyperlipidemia, and stroke presented to the emergency department with altered mental status. She was conversational and alert but oriented only to herself. Her son reported that she had been acting confused before admission. On initial examination, she was found to have a temperature of 39.3  C, heart rate of 107, respiratory rate of 27, BP 156/97, and oxygen saturation of 91% at room air.
