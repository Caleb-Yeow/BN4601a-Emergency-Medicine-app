A 71-year-old female presented to the emergency department (ED) with left leg pain, swelling and fever for 1 day. It was preceded by recent unroofing of the nail bed of the great toe. She had poorly controlled type II diabetes mellitus, hypertension and coronary artery disease. In the ED, she was febrile (temperature 103.1 F) with blood pressure of 188/120 mm Hg and pulse of 106/min.
