We report the case of a 38-year-old obese female with a body mass index (BMI) of 35, with no known comorbidity. She reported diarrhea accompanied by fever three weeks before, which revealed a COVID-19 infection, for which she was treated at home without complications. She went to the emergency room on the seventeenth day after the onset of symptoms, with sudden onset dyspnea. The physical examination revealed: Glasgow coma scale 15/15; blood pressure: 127/80mmhg; heart rate: 100 beats/min; respiratory rate: 30 cycles/min; 87% SaO2 in ambient air; crackling and wheezing rales at the base of the left lung.
